# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Lucide, Zustand, Dexie/IndexedDB, Zod, dnd-kit, Vitest, Playwright; deploy target Vercel. Specified by the user in the master brief.

## Users

Lead audience: solo founders and independent makers planning one product at a time. Also freelancers/consultants, startup founders, small product teams and product managers.

## Product Purpose

Ordo is a lightweight, browser-first product roadmap builder: visual roadmap creation, basic strategy planning (objectives, initiatives, features), lightweight prioritisation, and roadmap communication/export. Success: a solo founder can go from blank to a shareable roadmap without registering or learning a methodology.

## Positioning

Local-first and account-free: the roadmap lives in the user's browser (IndexedDB), with backup/restore under the user's control. No mandatory registration and no AI dependency in V1.

## Operating Context

Desktop-first browser app, used in focused planning sessions. One dataset (product, objectives, initiatives, features, milestones) rendered through multiple views.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Versioned data schema with migrations; soft deletion; backup and restore.
- No external database or authentication in V1; free-first infrastructure.
- No hardcoded demonstration data in production.
- Phased delivery: Phase 0 is foundation only (no roadmap features yet).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ordo. Logo is a text wordmark "Ordo" for now (placeholder until a mark exists).
- Tagline: "Plan better. Build smarter".

## Product Principles

1. Simple before sophisticated.
2. Strategy without unnecessary complexity; progressive disclosure.
3. One dataset, multiple visualisations.
4. Local-first data ownership; never lose the user's work.
5. Every control works; no fake buttons.
